Why it matters

Outreach is how most B2B businesses find new clients, and doing it properly is slow: finding the right businesses, checking each one, finding an email address and writing something that doesn't read like spam. That's 15 to 20 minutes per lead, so it's always the first thing to drop.

We set up a system that does the research for you and leaves the conversation to you. It searches for your ideal clients, checks the signal that matters for what you sell, finds the right contact, sends a short personal email and one polite follow-up, catches every reply and drafts your answer. It's the same system we built and run for Astra Nexora's own outreach.

FAQ

Questions about Lead Generation Automation.

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll reply.

Ask on WhatsApp
01Is this spam?

No. The system sends a small number of personal emails a day, each about the prospect's own business, with one follow-up and an easy opt-out. Anyone who says no is never contacted again.

02What does it cost to run?

Nothing per month. It runs on free Google Apps Script, Gmail, Sheets and other free tools. You pay once for the setup.

03Does it reply to clients by itself?

No. Replies come to you with a suggested answer already drafted. You read it, edit if needed and send it.

04Will it work for my industry?

If you sell to other businesses, usually yes. The search, the checks and the email wording are set up for your industry.

05Can I see it working?

Yes. We run the same system for Astra Nexora's own outreach, and our case study shows the real dashboards and emails.
