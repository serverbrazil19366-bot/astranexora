Website design cost in Tamil Nadu: what you'll pay and why

How much does a business website cost in Tamil Nadu? What affects the price, WordPress vs Wix vs custom, what's included and hidden costs to ask about.

Key takeaways

  • The price depends on the number of pages, design, features and who writes the content.
  • WordPress, Wix and custom builds each suit different needs and budgets.
  • Ask about domain, hosting, maintenance and SEO, which are often extra.
  • A cheap website that doesn't bring enquiries costs more in the long run.

A website is usually the first thing a customer checks before calling you. But when you ask for quotes, you may hear anything from a few thousand rupees to a few lakhs. Here's what actually decides the cost of a website in Tamil Nadu, and how to make sure you get one that brings enquiries.

01What affects the cost of a website

Number of pages

A simple 5-page site (home, about, services, gallery, contact) costs less than a site with a page for every service, location pages and a blog. More pages usually also mean better SEO.

Design: template or custom

A ready-made template is quicker and cheaper. A custom design built around your brand takes longer but looks more professional and is easier to make fast.

Features

Booking forms, WhatsApp integration, online payments, product catalogues, multiple languages and dashboards all add work.

Content

Who writes the text and supplies the photos? Professional copywriting and photography make a big difference to results and to the price.

02WordPress vs Wix vs custom code

  • WordPress: flexible, huge plugin library, easy to grow into a large site. Needs regular updates and good hosting.
  • Wix: quick to set up and easy to edit yourself, with hosting included. Less flexible for complex features.
  • Custom code: fastest and most flexible, ideal for performance and unique designs, but you rely on a developer for changes.

The right choice depends on how often you'll update the site and what it needs to do.

03What should be included

  • Mobile-first, responsive design
  • Fast loading on mobile networks
  • Clear calls to action: call, WhatsApp and enquiry form on every page
  • Basic on-page SEO: titles, descriptions, headings and a sitemap
  • Google Analytics and Search Console set up
  • SSL certificate (https)
  • Training or a short guide on how to update your site

04Hidden costs to ask about

  1. Domain name, renewed every year.
  2. Hosting, usually billed yearly.
  3. Maintenance: updates, backups and security, especially for WordPress.
  4. Business email on your domain.
  5. SEO and content after launch.
  6. Changes after launch: are small edits included?

05Cheap vs good value

A cheap website that loads slowly, looks outdated or hides your phone number can cost you customers every day. A good website pays for itself through the enquiries it brings. When comparing quotes, ask each designer to show sites they've built and how those sites help their clients get enquiries.

06Before you request quotes

Write down your goal (calls, bookings, sales), the pages you need, the features you want and two or three websites you like. You'll get more accurate quotes and avoid surprises. Our website audit checklist is a good place to start if you already have a site.

08Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to build a business website?

A small business website usually takes a few weeks. Larger sites with many pages or custom features take longer.

Is WordPress or Wix better for a small business?

Wix is easier to manage yourself; WordPress is more flexible as you grow. We help you choose based on your needs.

Will my website rank on Google automatically?

No. A good website is the foundation, but ranking needs SEO: the right pages, content and local signals.

Do I own my website?

You should. Make sure the domain, hosting and website files are in your name.
