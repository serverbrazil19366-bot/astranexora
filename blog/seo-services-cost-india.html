SEO services cost in India: what you should pay in 2026

How much do SEO services cost in India? What decides the price, monthly vs one-time SEO, what should be included and how to spot cheap SEO that does harm.

Key takeaways

  • SEO pricing depends on your competition, the size of your website and how much content is needed.
  • Monthly SEO is the norm because results build over time; a one-time audit is a good first step.
  • A proper SEO service includes technical fixes, on-page work, content, local SEO and reporting.
  • Very cheap SEO often means automated links or copied content, which can hurt your site.

"How much does SEO cost?" is one of the first questions every business owner asks, and the honest answer is: it depends. In India you will see quotes ranging from a few thousand rupees a month to several lakhs. This guide explains what decides the price, what you should get for your money and how to compare quotes.

01What decides the cost of SEO

01

How competitive your market is

Ranking a clinic in a small town is very different from ranking an online store against national brands. The more competition for your keywords, the more work is needed.

02

The size and state of your website

A 10-page website with few problems needs less work than a 500-product store with technical issues. An audit shows how much fixing is needed before growth work can start.

03

Local or national targeting

Local SEO for one location is usually more affordable than ranking across India. Multiple branches or cities add work.

04

How much content you need

New service pages, location pages and blog posts take research and writing. Content is often the biggest part of an SEO budget.

02Monthly SEO vs one-time SEO

One-time SEO usually means an audit and a round of fixes: titles, descriptions, speed, indexing and structure. It is a great starting point, especially for a new website.

Monthly SEO is how most businesses work with an agency, because rankings build over months. Each month includes new content, improvements, local SEO and reporting.

Many businesses start with a one-time audit and fixes, then move to a monthly plan once the foundations are right.

03What should be included in an SEO service

  • Technical SEO: indexing, speed, mobile usability, structured data and fixing errors in Google Search Console.
  • On-page SEO: titles, descriptions, headings and content written around what customers search.
  • Content: service pages, location pages and blog posts that answer real questions.
  • Local SEO: Google Business Profile optimisation, reviews and consistent business details.
  • Links and mentions: genuine listings, partnerships and PR, not bulk link packages.
  • Reporting: clicks, rankings and, most importantly, the enquiries SEO brings.

04Why very cheap SEO is risky

If a package promises hundreds of backlinks or first-page rankings for a tiny monthly fee, be careful. These packages often rely on spam links, copied content or automated tools. At best they do nothing; at worst they get your site penalised and you pay again to clean it up.

05How to compare SEO quotes

  1. Ask each agency for a written list of monthly deliverables.
  2. Check whether content writing is included and how many pages per month.
  3. Ask how they build links and get mentions.
  4. Ask what reports you will receive and how often.
  5. Make sure your website and Google accounts stay in your name.

06Is SEO worth the cost?

For most businesses that rely on Google, yes. Unlike ads, the traffic SEO brings doesn't stop when you stop paying, and good pages keep bringing enquiries for years. Our guide on SEO vs Google Ads explains when each makes more sense.

08Frequently asked questions

How much do SEO services cost per month in India?

It varies widely with competition, website size and content needs. Compare quotes on the deliverables included, not just the monthly figure.

How long does SEO take to work?

Usually three to six months for strong results, sometimes sooner for local searches and technical fixes.

Can I pay for SEO once and stop?

A one-time audit and fixes can help a lot, but competitors keep improving, so ongoing content and optimisation keep you ahead.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No honest agency can guarantee rankings. We commit to a clear plan, real work and transparent reporting.
