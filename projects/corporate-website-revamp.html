Corporate Website Revamp

A legacy corporate website redesigned as a modern lead-generation site while protecting its existing search rankings.

Concept project. This shows how we would approach this kind of brief. It is not a client case study and has no client results.

The brief

The challenge.

The company's old website still ranked for valuable searches, but looked outdated and produced few enquiries. A redesign risked losing those rankings if URLs and content changed carelessly.

Our approach

How we'd solve it.

Four steps, each with a clear outcome before the next one starts.

  1. 01

    Audit what works

    Find the pages, keywords and links that already bring traffic.

  2. 02

    Redesign with care

    Modern design and clearer journeys, keeping valuable content and URLs.

  3. 03

    Migrate safely

    Redirect map, metadata transfer and pre-launch checks.

  4. 04

    Convert better

    Stronger calls to action, case studies and simple contact paths.

Scope

What we'd deliver.

Part of our Website Design & Development service.

  • SEO and content audit
  • Redirect and migration plan
  • New page designs
  • Rebuilt website
  • Post-launch monitoring report
Success measures

What success looks like.

The goals we'd set and report against for a project like this.

  • 01

    Keep existing rankings through the migration

  • 02

    Cut mobile load time significantly

  • 03

    Make the enquiry path two clicks or fewer

  • 04

    Measure leads from every page
