Google Business Profile: set it up and get found on Maps

A plain guide to setting up and improving your Google Business Profile: categories, services, photos, reviews, posts and the mistakes that cost you calls.

S. Saktheeswaran28 Oct 2026 · 3 min read
On this page
  1. Setting up your profile
  2. Keeping it active
  3. Common mistakes to avoid
  4. Frequently asked questions
Share

Key takeaways

  • Your Business Profile is often seen before your website, so treat it like a shop front.
  • The primary category matters most; pick the most specific one that fits.
  • Real photos, complete services and fast replies to reviews make a visible difference.
  • Service businesses can hide their address and show a service area instead.

For many people, your Google Business Profile is the first thing they see about you. It shows your rating, photos, hours and a call button, often before they ever visit your website. A complete, active profile can bring calls and WhatsApp messages every week. An empty one sends those people to a competitor.

Here is how to set it up properly and keep it working for you.

01Setting up your profile

01

Create or claim the profile

Go to business.google.com, search for your business name and claim it if it exists, or create a new one. Use your real business name exactly as it appears on your signboard and website. Adding keywords to the name breaks Google's guidelines.

02

Choose the right primary category

This is the most important setting. Pick the most specific category that describes your main service, for example "Dental clinic" rather than "Health". Add a few secondary categories only if they are genuinely part of your business.

03

Address or service area

If customers visit you, add your address. If you travel to customers or work online, choose to hide your address and add the areas you serve instead. Either way, verify the profile when Google asks, usually by video or a code.

04

Fill in everything else

Add your phone number, website, opening hours, services with short descriptions, and a clear business description. Every empty field is a missed chance to match what people search for.

02Keeping it active

05

Add real photos regularly

Upload photos of your premises, team, products and work. Real photos build more trust than stock images, and an active profile looks like an active business.

06

Ask for reviews and reply to all of them

Share your review link with happy customers on WhatsApp after a job is done. Reply to every review: thank people for good ones, and answer bad ones calmly with a way to fix the problem. Future customers read your replies.

07

Post updates and offers

Use posts to share offers, new services, events or recent work. They keep your profile fresh and give people another reason to contact you.

08

Answer questions

People can ask questions on your profile. Answer them, and add common questions yourself with clear answers.

03Common mistakes to avoid

  • Stuffing keywords or the city name into your business name.
  • Using a different phone number or address from your website.
  • Picking a broad primary category that doesn't match your main service.
  • Ignoring reviews, especially negative ones.
  • Creating more than one profile for the same business location.

04Frequently asked questions

Is Google Business Profile free?

Yes. Creating and managing a profile is free. Google also sells ads that can appear in Maps, but they are optional.

Why is my business not showing on Google Maps?

Common reasons are an unverified profile, a suspended profile, a category that doesn't match the search, or strong competitors closer to the searcher. Check the profile status first.

How do I get more reviews?

Ask at the right moment, right after a happy customer interaction, and make it easy with a direct review link sent on WhatsApp.
