Why it matters

WhatsApp is where your customers and partners actually read messages, but most businesses either send broadcasts from a phone (which only reach people who saved your number) or pay a third-party platform every month plus a markup on every message.

We set up your WhatsApp Business API account on the official Meta Cloud API and build a campaign platform you own: personalised approved templates, clean contact lists, safe sending that protects your number, and a dashboard that tracks delivery, reads and replies. You pay Meta's message fees, not a monthly subscription.

FAQ

Questions about WhatsApp Business API Marketing.

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll reply.

Ask on WhatsApp
01Is this the official WhatsApp API?

Yes. We use Meta's own WhatsApp Cloud API with a verified business account and approved templates.

02How much does it cost to send messages?

You pay Meta's per-message fees directly. There's no monthly platform subscription; you pay us once for the setup.

03Will my number get banned?

We use approved templates, clean contact lists, small batches and gradual warm-up to protect your number, and only message relevant contacts.

04Can customers reply?

Yes. Replies and button clicks are routed to your team's WhatsApp number so a person answers.

05Can I see an example?

Yes. We built this for ProPedoZR, which messaged 150 dentists on day one with zero failures. The case study is on our founder's portfolio.
